# Reference Image Prompts

Paste these into your image generator (for example GPT image gen in ChatGPT). Generate 2 or 3 variants of each prompt and pick the best one. Save picks into this folder with the exact filename given, as PNG or JPG.

**Naming:** `<preset>__<bookmark>.png`. The preset is `dusk` or `night`. The bookmark matches a camera bookmark in the capture script, so each capture is compared against its reference. If you keep more than one pick, add `__2`, `__3`.

**How they are used:** as mood, value structure and color targets, not pixel matches. The 3D build has to beat them, not copy them.

**Aspect:** landscape 3:2 (1536x1024) for all of them.

## Shared style block

Append this to the end of every prompt:

> Photorealistic cinematic film still, shot on a large-sensor camera, natural lens, real physical scale, high dynamic range, rich shadow detail, no text, no watermark, no logos, no borders, no people visible unless stated.

## Milestone 1: sea and sky only (no ship)

**1. `dusk__sea_low.png`**
> Eye level two meters above the surface of the open North Atlantic in a violent storm at dusk. Steep dark green-grey swells taller than a house roll toward the camera. Crests break into white water, and wind tears spindrift off them in long horizontal streaks. Streaked foam lies in lines along the wind across every wave face. A low copper sun bleeds through a narrow gap under a heavy slate cloud deck and backlights the spray, making the thin wave crests glow translucent green. Rain slants across the frame.

**2. `night__sea_low_flash.png`**
> Eye level two meters above a raging ocean at night during a violent storm, lit for an instant by a lightning flash inside the clouds. The flash reveals huge black-green swells, breaking crests, streaks of white foam and spindrift frozen mid-air, and driving rain caught as bright streaks. The sky is a churning low cloud deck lit from within, cold blue-white near the flash and fading to near black at the edges of the frame.

**3. `night__sea_low_dark.png`**
> The same storm at night between lightning flashes. Almost black, with only a faint cold glow from the clouds. The shapes of the swells read barely, from faint sheen on the wet wave faces and from the pale foam streaks, which stay the brightest thing in frame. Heavy rain, and a sense of enormous dark motion. Subtle, moody, low key, but not muddy: the foam patterns stay legible.

**4. `dusk__sea_high.png`**
> Looking out across a storm sea from 25 meters up, as if from the top of a ship's mast, at dusk. Rank after rank of long, heavy swells march to a hazy horizon, their backs streaked with white foam lines aligned with the wind. Whitecaps are everywhere. Curtains of rain hang under a dark cloud deck, with one copper-lit gap near the horizon where the low sun breaks through and lays a glittering path on the water.

**5. `night__horizon_lightning.png`**
> A storm horizon at night over the open ocean, seen from just above the waves. A branching lightning bolt strikes the sea in the middle distance, and its light spreads through the cloud base and reflects as a broken bright path on the heaving water. The rain curtains between camera and bolt are visible as grey veils. Big swells fill the foreground, with foam and spray catching the flash.

**6. `dusk__storm_sky.png`**
> A wide storm sky over the ocean at dusk. A dark, heavy, textured cloud deck sits low, with ragged scud racing beneath it. There is a long gap of glowing copper and pale gold light near the horizon, and rain shafts falling from the cloud base. A narrow band of rough grey-green sea runs along the bottom of the frame.

## Milestones 2 to 4: ship and rigging

**7. `dusk__ship_wide.png`**
> A two-masted brigantine (square-rigged foremast, fore-and-aft gaff mainsail) running before a violent storm at dusk, seen from off her port quarter. She is heeled hard and surfing down the face of a huge swell, bow buried in white water and spray exploding off the weather bow. Reefed topsail, torn canvas flogging. Dark tarred hull, weathered wood, and wet rigging drawn in sharp lines against a copper gap in the clouds. Tiny and alone in an enormous sea.

**8. `night__deck_bow.png`**
> On the deck of a small wooden brigantine at night in a storm, looking forward from near the helm toward the bow. A wall of green water is breaking over the bow and pouring along the deck in a foaming torrent. A single hanging oil lantern throws warm light onto wet planks, ropes and belaying pins. Beyond the bow is black sea, rain streaking through the lantern light, and a distant lightning flash outlining the foremast rigging.

**9. `dusk__masthead_down.png`**
> Looking steeply down from the foretop of a brigantine in a storm at dusk. Below are the wet deck far beneath, the ratlines of the shrouds dropping away, and the yard and furled sail. The ship is heeled so far that the churning sea fills half the frame beside the hull. Spray and rain fill the air, with a copper light from the horizon. Strong vertigo.

**10. `night__yard_reefing.png`**
> A lone sailor in an oilskin coat stands on the footrope of a topsail yard high above a storm sea at night, fighting to tie in a reef in a flogging sail. Lightning behind the clouds backlights the sailor and the rigging in hard rim light. Rain whips sideways, and the ship's deck and the heaving foam-streaked sea are far below. The sailor is seen from behind and at a distance, face not visible.

## Milestone 5: lighthouse and ending

**11. `night__lighthouse_far.png`**
> From the deck of a ship at sea at night in a storm, a distant lighthouse on a dark headland about three kilometers away. Its rotating beam sweeps through rain and sea haze as a solid visible shaft of light. Big swells and foam in the foreground catch the faint beam, and the cloud deck is lit by a far lightning flash. The lighthouse is small in frame but is clearly the focal point and the goal.

**12. `night__harbor_entrance.png`**
> A narrow harbor entrance at night in a storm, seen from the helm of a small sailing ship approaching from the sea. A lighthouse on the headland at left sweeps its beam overhead. Two small leading lights on shore line up to mark the channel. Huge breakers explode white on rocks on both sides of a gap of darker, slightly calmer water. Rain, spray, and the ship's bowsprit visible in the lower foreground.

## Selection checklist

When picking between variants, prefer the one where:
- Wave scale reads as enormous and heavy, not like a lake.
- Foam has structure (streaks, lines and patches), not uniform white noise.
- Darks keep detail and the image is not muddy grey.
- Lighting direction and color are clear, with one strong idea per image.
